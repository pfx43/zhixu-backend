## 第 12 页

77. There was a great poet of the Tang Dynasty whose poems are regarded as "history
of poetry." Who is the poet?
Answer: Du Fu.
78. Who is the writer of the long narrative poems Changhenge and Pipa Xing?
Answer: Bai Juyi.
79. What is the name of a novel in which the Monkey King is the main character? Who
is its author?
Answer: Journey to the West, Wu Cheng'en.
80. The classical Chinese novel Journey to the West written in the vernacular is ba
sed on the story of Monk Xuanzang's pilgrimage to the western Tianzhu in search of
the Buddhist scriptures. Which place does "Tianzhu" refer to?
A.Ancient Arabia
B. Ancient India
C. Ancient Persia,
D. Ancient Rome
Answer: B. Ancient India.
81. Wu Song and Song Jiang are characters in a famous novel, what is the name of th
e novel? Who is the writer of the novel?
Answer: Outlaws of the Marshes, Shi Nai'an.
82.What is the name of a novel in which Zhu Geliang and Cao Cao are characters?
Answer: The Romance of the Three Kingdoms.
83. What was the first novel written in the vernacular in the modern history of Chi
nese literature? What is the name of the author?
Answer: The Diary of a Madman, by Lu Xun.
84. In which novel did Lu Xun depict an intellectual who was deeply influenced and
defiled by the feudal civil service recruitment examination system and eventually h
ad to make a living on stealing books?
Answer: Kong Yiji.
85. Who is the writer of the saga novels, The Rickshaw Boy, Four Generations under
One Roof, and the dramas Teahouse, Longxugou, etc.?
Answer: Lao She.
