117. Please point out the playing forms of the following Chinese Folk Musical Instr
uments according to the playing ways of blowing, pulling, plucking and striking or
beating:
Xiao, Flute, Suona, Erhu, Jinghu, Banhu, Guzheng, Guqin, Pipa, gong, and Drum
Answer: Blowing —— Xiao, Flute, Suona
Pulling —— Erhu, Jinghu, Banhu
Plucking —— Guzheng, Guqin, Pipa
Striking —— gong, drum
118. The performers of Peking Opera are classified into four categories of roles: S
heng, Dan, Jing and Chou, please link each of them up to their respective role they
play.
Sheng (生)
male role
Dan (旦)
female role
Jing (净)
Painted face; male role, a man of virility or known as hualian
Chou (丑)
male role, a clown, a man of humorous or nimble-witted or deceitful
and insidious character
Answer: Sheng ———— Male role (laosheng, bearded or middle-aged gentleman; xiao
sheng, young scholar, and wusheng, a military man or valiant)
Dan ———— Female role (qingyi, young or middle-aged woman; huadan, a perky, bold
and unconventional woman; wudan, a military maiden; laodan, an old woman)
Jing ———— Painted face: Male role, a man of virility, also known as hualian
Chou ———— Male role, a man of humorous or nimble-witted or deceitful and insidi
ous character.
