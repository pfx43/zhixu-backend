## 第 10 页

64. What is "帛书"? (Picture: Book copied on silk)
Answer: It is the book written on the silk by ancient Chinese.
65. Who made China's first airplane?
Answer: Feng Ru (1883-1912)，born in Enping, Guangdong. He designed and produced an
airplane and succeeded in a test fly in 1909.
66. One way of traditional Chinese medicine to treat patients is to put filiform ne
edles into their body according to certain acupoints. What method is it called?
Answer: Acupuncture and moxibustion.

in the Han Dynasty who was honored by later generations as a "Master Doctor"?
