## 第 11 页

72. China's first anthology of ancient poetry is __________; with 305 poems collect
ed, it is also known as "Three Hundred Poems."
Answer: Shi Jing. (The Book of Songs)
73. What is the typical work of the great poet Qu Yuan during the Warring States pe
riod?
Answer: Li Sao, Chuci. (The Lamenti, The elegies of Chu.).
74. Fill in the blank: China boasts a wealth of ancient mythology, of which the lar
gest number of mythologies appeared in the ___________.
Answer: Shan Hai Jing. (The Classic of Mountains and Rivers)
75. In each stage of its development, Chinese literature boasted a specific type of
literary form marking the highest achievement of its own. Please connect with line
s the following literary forms with their corresponding stage.
Han Dynasty
Poetry
Tang Dynasty
Prose-poetry (赋)
Song Dynasty
Zaju (杂剧)
Ming & Qing dynasties
Ci (词)
Yuan Dynasty
Novel
Answer:
Han Dynasty ———— prose-poetry
Tang Dynasty ———— Poetry
Song Dynasty ———— Ci
Ming & Qing dynasties ———— novel
Yuan Dynasty ———— Zaju
76. There was a great poet of the Tang Dynasty known as a "banished immortal" poet.
Who is he?
Answer: Li Bai.
