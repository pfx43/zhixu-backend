109. There was a famous calligrapher honored as the "Great Master Calligrapher" in
the Jin Dynasty who had written the Preface to the Orchid Pavilion Collection (兰亭
序). What was his name?
Answer: Wang Xizhi.
110. Who were the four famous script calligraphers of ancient China?
Answer: Ouyang Xun, Yan Zhenqing, Liu Gongquan, and Zhao Mengfu.
111. The Sui and Tang dynasties saw the zenith of classical painting in China, toge
ther with a number of talented painters coming forth, who of them was honored as th
e "Master Painter" of the Tang Dynasty?
Answer: Wu Daozi.
112. The best-known Festival of Pure Brightness on the River (清明上河图) presents
a vivid picture of the bustling marketplace on the banks of the Bian River of Kaife
ng city, reflecting every aspect of the social life in the Northern Song Dynasty. W
ho was its painter?
Answer: Zhang Zeduan.
